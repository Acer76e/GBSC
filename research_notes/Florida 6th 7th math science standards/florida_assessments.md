## 1. FAST Mathematics Grades 6 and 7: windows, format, calculators, reference sheets, achievement levels

### Takeaway
In 2026-27 Florida 6th and 7th graders take FAST Mathematics three times. PM1 runs Aug 3 to Sep 25, 2026, PM2 runs Nov 30, 2026 to Jan 22, 2027, and PM3 runs May 3 to 28, 2027. Each is a computer-adaptive test with an on-screen calculator (four-function in Grade 6, scientific in Grade 7), an online reference sheet and scratch paper. Only PM3 counts for accountability, and Level 3 (on grade level) starts at a scale score of 229 in Grade 6 and 235 in Grade 7.

### Cited Findings
**Testing windows, 2026-27 (official):**
- PM1: August 3 to September 25, 2026. PM2: November 30, 2026 to January 22, 2027. PM3 for Grades 3-10: May 3 to May 28, 2027. (PM3 for K-2 is earlier: April 12 to May 28, 2027.) — [FDOE 2026-27 FAST 3-10 Fact Sheet (Aug 2026)](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2627FASTGrd310FS.pdf); [FDOE 2026-27 FAST K-2 Fact Sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2627FASTK2FS.pdf); [FDOE 2026-2027 Statewide Assessment Schedule (dated May 26, 2026)](https://www.fldoe.org/file/5663/2627StatewideAssessmentSched.pdf)
- The statewide schedule dated May 26, 2026 replaced a December 2024 preliminary version. A third-party study guide gives the PM1 start as August 10, 2026, which conflicts with the official August 3. Use the FDOE date. — [FDOE schedule](https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/5663/urlt/2627StatewideAssessmentSched.pdf); conflicting: [open-exam-prep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/fast-ela-grade6/introduction-overview/study-strategies-pm-plan)
- PM1 and PM2 are informational (progress monitoring). PM3 is used for school accountability. Districts and schools pick the actual test days inside each window. — [FDOE 2026-27 FAST 3-10 Fact Sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2627FASTGrd310FS.pdf) (per search summary)
- For comparison, the 2027-28 schedule has already been posted. — [FDOE 2027-28 schedule](https://origin.fldoe.org/file/5663/2728StatewideAssessmentSched.pdf) (not reviewed)

**Format:**
- FAST Grades 3-8 Mathematics (and Grades 3-10 ELA Reading) are computer-adaptive tests (CATs). Students get an overall scale score and achievement level, plus a performance level for each reporting category. — [FDOE Understanding Florida Statewide Assessments Reports for Families (Sept 2024)](https://flfast.org/content/contentresources/en/Understanding%20Florida%20Statewide%20Assessments%20Reports_For%20Families_FINAL.pdf)
- Test length for Grades 6-8 FAST Mathematics is 36-40 items. — [FDOE Test Design Summary and Blueprint: FAST Mathematics and B.E.S.T. EOCs (updated Feb 5, 2025)](https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/20102/urlt/TDS-FAST-Math.pdf) (per search summary)
- Duval County lists the Grades 6-8 FAST math average testing time on its calendar. The science figure from that calendar is in section 3. — [Duval Schools state/district calendars](https://www.duvalschools.org/o/dcps/page/state-and-district-assessment-calendars)

**Calculator policy, 2026-27:**
- Grade 6 uses a four-function calculator. Grades 7 and 8 use a scientific calculator. Both are built into the testing platform. Handheld calculators are allowed only if the district chooses to allow or provide them. — [FDOE 2026-27 FAST 3-10 Fact Sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2627FASTGrd310FS.pdf)
- The July 2026 calculator policy lists approved handheld four-function calculators for Grade 6 (for example TI-108, Casio HS-4G, Sharp EL233SB) and approved scientific calculators for Grades 7-8. — [FDOE Calculator and Reference Sheet Policies (updated July 2026)](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/FSACalcRefSheetPolicy.pdf)

**Reference sheets and scratch paper:**
- Online mathematics reference sheets are available in the testing platform for Grades 4-8. All FAST Mathematics test-takers get scratch paper. — [FDOE 2026-27 FAST 3-10 Fact Sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2627FASTGrd310FS.pdf); [FDOE Statewide Assessments Guide 2026-2027 (updated Sept 2026)](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/K12SAG.pdf)
- The FAST/B.E.S.T. Mathematics Reference Sheets Packet (2025-26) has the Grade 6 and Grade 7 sheets, and schools may print copies. — [flfast.org 2025-26 Reference Sheets Packet](https://flfast.org/content/contentresources/en/FL_2025-26_FAST-BEST-Mathematics-Reference-Sheets-Packet_Final.pdf)

**Achievement levels and scale scores (B.E.S.T. scale):**
- Grade 6 Math: Level 1 168-212, Level 2 213-228, **Level 3 229-238**, Level 4 239-253, Level 5 254-287. — [FDOE 2026-27 FAST 3-10 Fact Sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2627FASTGrd310FS.pdf) (ranges as summarized from the fact sheet, matching [Tutero](https://www.tutero.com/us/blog/florida-fast-test-guide))
- Grade 7 Math: Level 1 175-222, Level 2 223-234, **Level 3 235-246**, Level 4 247-257, Level 5 258-288. — [FDOE 2026-27 FAST 3-10 Fact Sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2627FASTGrd310FS.pdf); [testpreploom](https://www.testpreploom.com/blog/fast-score-chart-by-grade)
- Level 3 and above means on grade level. Level 5 is described as "Exemplary. Highly likely to excel in the next grade." The 2025-26 fact sheet lists the same Grade 6/7 ranges. — [FDOE 2026-27 FAST fact sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2627FASTGrd310FS.pdf); [FDOE 2025-26 FAST fact sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/2526FASTGrd310FS.pdf)
- Ignore the old FSA-scale numbers that still appear online (for example Grade 6 Level 3 = 325-338, Grade 7 = 330-345). FAST/B.E.S.T. replaced that scale. — outdated: [study.com](https://study.com/academy/popular/fsa-test-score-information.html), [algebro.ai](https://algebro.ai/blog/what-is-a-passing-fast-score)

### Inferences
- Because the test is adaptive, a study app should adapt difficulty too, and should spend extra practice on the student's weakest reporting category, since parents see category-level results.
- The app's calculator tool should match the test: four-function only in Grade 6 and scientific in Grade 7. Grade 6 practice should not assume a scientific calculator.
- Good check-in points for an app are just before each window: late August/September (PM1), December/January (PM2) and late April/May (PM3).

### Gaps
- I could not read session structure or time limits for Grades 6-7 math in the fact sheet itself because the fetch was blocked. (FAST math for Grades 6-8 has generally been one session, but that is not confirmed here.)
- I could not confirm whether the Grade 6 calculator is available on every item or only on some. Older FAST policy had calculator-neutral items, so check the July 2026 policy PDF.

## 2. Blueprint weights by reporting category, Grade 6 and Grade 7

### Takeaway
Grade 6 FAST Math has three reporting categories, with Number Sense and Operations the largest. Grade 7 has four categories weighted roughly evenly, with Number Sense/Algebraic Reasoning slightly heavier. The official source is FDOE's "Test Design Summary and Blueprint: FAST Mathematics and B.E.S.T. EOCs" (last update seen: Feb 5, 2025). The Grade 6 ranges are well supported. The Grade 7 ranges come from a third-party summary and still need checking.

### Cited Findings
- **Grade 6** (FDOE blueprint):
  - Number Sense and Operations: **33-42%**
  - Algebraic Reasoning: **25-36%**
  - Geometric Reasoning, Data Analysis, and Probability: **25-36%**
  - Sources: [FDOE TDS FAST Math](https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/20102/urlt/TDS-FAST-Math.pdf); same ranges in the [Oviedo/OCSD-hosted copy, 6th Grade FAST blueprint](https://files-backend.assets.thrillshare.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/2897/Ocsd/75c8e332-42ba-4045-bc83-1ff59983a34d/6th_Grade_FAST_1.pdf?disposition=inline) and the 2022 FAST score-report guide ([Understanding FAST Reports](https://basicsolutionsgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Understanding-FAST-Reports-For-Families.pdf))
  - The 2025 family guide names the third category without "Measurement", so category labels vary slightly between documents. — [Understanding FSA Reports for Families 2025-26](https://flfast.org/content/contentresources/en/Understanding%20FSA%20Reports%20for%20Families_25-26_FINAL_080725.pdf)
- **Grade 7** category names (official): Number Sense and Operations and Algebraic Reasoning; Proportional Reasoning and Relationships; Geometric Reasoning; Data Analysis and Probability. — [FDOE family reports guide (Sept 2024)](https://flfast.org/content/contentresources/en/Understanding%20Florida%20Statewide%20Assessments%20Reports_For%20Families_FINAL.pdf)
- **Grade 7** weights (**third-party only, unverified**):
  - Number Sense and Operations and Algebraic Reasoning: 25-31%
  - Proportional Reasoning and Relationships: 22-31%
  - Geometric Reasoning: 22-28%
  - Data Analysis and Probability: 22-28%
  - Source: [open-exam-prep 2026-27 parent guide](https://open-exam-prep.com/blog/florida-fast-test-2026-27-parent-guide-pm1-pm2-pm3-scores). The same site's Grade 3 list did not match FDOE's, so check it against the FDOE blueprint. The official 2025-26 Statewide Assessments Guide shows a 22-28% figure in a garbled table, which loosely supports these ranges. — [FDOE K12SAG](https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/20102/urlt/K12SAG.pdf)
- Do not use the old FSA Grade 7 weights (25/21/23/16% under categories such as "Ratio and Proportional Relationships"). They do not apply to FAST. — [FDOE FSA Math TDS (legacy)](https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/5663/urlt/TDS-FSAMath.pdf)
- FDOE also publishes Mathematics Reporting Category Statements, which describe what each Grade 6 and Grade 7 category covers. — [FDOE Mathematics Reporting Category Statements](https://cdn.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/20102/urlt/Mathematics-Reporting-Category-Statements_FINAL_508.pdf)

### Inferences
- In Grade 6, roughly 33-42% of the test is fractions, decimals, integers and rational-number operations, so the app should weight practice that way. In Grade 7, proportional reasoning (ratios, percent, scale) is its own category of about a quarter of the test.
- The benchmark-level lists in the TDS (which B.E.S.T. MA.6.* and MA.7.* benchmarks map to each category) are the right skill tags for the app.

### Gaps
- The official Grade 7 percentages could not be read directly. Confirm them in the TDS PDF.
- I could not confirm whether a 2026-27 update to the TDS exists or whether the Feb 2025 version is still current.
- No separate public "Test Item Specifications" for FAST Grade 6/7 math turned up in search. FDOE appears to rely on the TDS, the reporting category statements and the B.E.S.T. benchmark clarifications instead. This is not confirmed.

## 3. Middle school science assessment: Grade 8 SSA (no state science test in Grades 6 or 7)

### Takeaway
Florida's only state science test in middle school is the Grade 8 Statewide Science Assessment (SSA), given in a May 3-28, 2027 window. It covers Grades 6-8 benchmarks: Nature of Science 19%, and Earth and Space, Physical and Life Science 27% each. There is no state science test in Grade 6 or 7. Any science testing in those grades is a district benchmark or quarterly assessment, or comes from curriculum vendors.

### Cited Findings
- Grade 8 SSA weights per the 2026-27 Statewide Assessments Guide: Nature of Science **19%**; Earth and Space Science **27%**; Physical Science **27%**; Life Science **27%**. — [FDOE Statewide Assessments Guide 2026-2027 (Sept 2026)](https://www.fldoe.org/file/20102/K12SAG.pdf)
- The Grade 8 Science Reporting Category Statements (May 2024) describe each category, including Nature of Science. — [FDOE Grade 8 Science RCS](https://cdn.fldoe.org/file/5663/Grade8-Science-rcs.pdf)
- Grade 8 Test Item Specifications (benchmarks covered, item types, content limits). — [FDOE SCIGR8TSI.pdf](https://origin.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/5663/urlt/SCIGR8TSI.pdf)
- Format: the 2025-26 science fact sheet describes the SSA as computer-based and adaptive. Results go into the Florida Reporting System within 24 hours of testing. Historically, all SSA items have been multiple choice. — [FDOE 2025-26 Statewide Science Assessments Fact Sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/5663/2526Gr5-8ScienceFactSheet.pdf); multiple choice: [FDOE NGSSS TDS (2016, old)](https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/5682/urlt/NGSSS-TDS-August16.pdf)
  - **Flag:** the snippet calling the test "computer-adaptive" came from a search summary. Earlier SSA documents describe a fixed-form computer-based test, so confirm in the fact sheet.
- 2026-27 window: Grades 5 and 8 Science, **May 3 to May 28, 2027**, with an average testing time of about 160 minutes. — [Uniform Statewide Assessment Calendar 2026-27, Rule 6A-1.094224 (district-hosted copy)](https://files.smartsites.parentsquare.com/9460/k12uniformassessmentcalendar2026-2027.pdf); [Duval Schools calendar](https://www.duvalschools.org/o/dcps/page/state-and-district-assessment-calendars). The 2025-26 window was May 1-29, 2026. — [FDOE 2025-26 science fact sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/5663/2526Gr5-8ScienceFactSheet.pdf)
  - **Flag:** one search summary read the FDOE schedule as listing Grades 5 & 8 Science on Aug 31 to Sep 25, 2026. That is probably a misread or a fall retake window, not the main administration. Several calendar copies agree on May 3-28, 2027.
- Grade 8 SSA scale and achievement levels (2025-26 fact sheet): Level 1 140-184, Level 2 185-202, Level 3 203-214, Level 4 215-224, Level 5 225-260. **Conflict:** the April 2026 family guide gives Level 2 as 185-199 and Level 3 as 200-214. — [FDOE 2025-26 science fact sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/5663/2526Gr5-8ScienceFactSheet.pdf); conflicting: [Understanding FSA Reports for Families (Apr 2026)](https://flfast.org/content/contentresources/en/Understanding%20FSA%20Reports%20for%20Families_25-26_FINAL_040126_508.pdf)
- 8th graders enrolled in Biology 1 take the Biology 1 EOC instead of the Grade 8 SSA. — [FDOE 2025-26 science fact sheet](https://www.fldoe.org/file/5663/2526Gr5-8ScienceFactSheet.pdf)
- There is a 2026-27 Science/Social Studies EOC fact sheet. — [FDOE 2627SciSSEOCFactSheet](http://origin.fldoe.org/file/5662/2627SciSSEOCFactSheet.pdf) (not reviewed)
- Grades 6 and 7: in district materials, the statewide science assessment appears only at Grade 8. State progress monitoring (FAST) covers ELA and math only. — [Broward testing presentation 2024-25](https://www.browardschools.com/cms/lib/FL01803656/Centricity/ModuleInstance/4372/testing%20community%20PPT%2024-25.pdf)

For example, Palm Beach's school progress-monitoring plan lists Grade 6/7/8 science benchmark assessments. — [Palm Beach PMP](https://www.palmbeachschools.org/cms/lib/FL50010848/Centricity/Domain/1921/FHHS%20School-wide%20PMP%20SY24.pdf)

### Inferences
- Content taught in Grades 6 and 7 science (in Comprehensive Science 1 and 2) shows up on the Grade 8 SSA. A study app should treat Grades 6-7 science as building toward the Grade 8 SSA blueprint, with all three content strands about equal and Nature of Science (experimental design, variables, data) running through every year.

## 4. How FAST results are used: accelerated math placement, Algebra 1 and Civics

### Takeaway
State law requires middle schoolers who want high school credit for Algebra 1 to take the Algebra 1 EOC (which counts 30% of the course grade), and Grade 7 Civics carries a state EOC. Decisions about who gets accelerated math (for example Grade 6 Advanced or Accelerated Math, then Algebra 1 in Grade 7 or 8) are mostly made by districts, which commonly use prior FAST math levels of 4 or 5. I found no single statewide FAST cut score for acceleration.

### Cited Findings
- Middle school students taking Algebra 1 for high school credit must take the statewide Algebra 1 EOC, and the EOC counts 30% of the final course grade. Passing the course does not depend on the EOC result. Middle school Civics also has an EOC worth 30% of the course grade. (These are mostly older statute and FAQ versions.) — [Fla. Stat. 1003.4156](https://florida.public.law/statutes/fla._stat._1003.4156); [2023 Florida Statutes 1003.4156](https://m.flsenate.gov/Statutes/1003.4156); [FDOE 2011 FAQ (old)](https://info.fldoe.org/docushare/dsweb/Get/Version-6643/dps-2011-46a.pdf)
- District example: Pinellas uses a Grade 4/5 FAST math Level 4 or 5 as the entry point to Grade 6 Accelerated Mathematics, with other routes through a virtual course or a competency test. — [Pinellas 2025-26 MS Math Options Parent Letter](https://www.pcsb.org/cms/lib/FL01903687/Centricity/Domain/161/25.26%20MS%20Math%20Options%20Parent%20Letter.pdf)
- The 2023 law CS/CS/SB 1430 revised the articulated acceleration mechanisms in s. 1007.27. I could not confirm whether it set middle school math placement rules. — [SB 1430 (2023) bill text](https://flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2023/1430/BillText/c2/HTML)

### Inferences
- Level 4-5 on Grade 5 or Grade 6 FAST math (Grade 6 Level 4 starts at 239, Grade 7 at 247) is the practical target for families aiming at Algebra 1 in Grade 7 or 8. Rules differ by district.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the current (2025/2026) text of s. 1003.4156 or whether passing Civics is still required for middle school promotion. The 2011 FAQ said yes, but it is old.
- I found no statewide rule tying FAST scores to placement. Families need their district's acceleration policy.
